About your teacher

Hi, I'm Priya.

I've spent the last fifteen years convinced that nobody is "bad at math" — they've just never had it explained the way their brain actually works. My job is to find that way, together with you.

I teach live over video with a full digital chalkboard, real-time annotation, and instant practice problems. No pre-recorded fluff — every class adapts to what you're stuck on that week.

Previously a curriculum lead at a national tutoring network, I now teach independently so every student gets a plan built around them, not a syllabus built for a classroom of thirty.
